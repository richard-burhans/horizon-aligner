## F1 -- C2: `estimate_hmm_params` uses an unstratified prefix sample, ascertainment-biased anchors, and is bypassed by `horizon_wga --graph`

- **Claim:** C2 (`docs/BENCHMARK_RESULTS.md:5`)
- **Verdict:** REFUTED
- **Flaw:**
  1. **Unstratified prefix sampling (`src/mdl/mod.rs:773-809`):** `estimate_hmm_params` iterates `lcbs_fwd` before `lcbs_rc`, takes only the first 64 anchors per LCB (`lcb.iter().take(64)` at line 775, `lcb.windows(2).take(64)` at line 794), and breaks out of the entire loop as soon as `total_bases >= 4000` (lines 790, 805). With 15-bp `MASK_PRIMARY_11_15` anchors, 4,000 bases is reached after ~267 anchors (at most 5 LCBs, or 1 LCB if ≥267 anchors). On whole chromosomes (`GRCh38.chr22`: 60 fwd / 4 rev LCBs; `chr6` 5 Mbp: 543 fwd / 355 rev LCBs), parameters are estimated exclusively from the first few forward LCBs, ignoring `lcbs_rc` and all downstream LCBs.
  2. **Ascertainment bias and indel censoring (`src/mdl/mod.rs:797,815-818`):** `identity_prior` is estimated from bases inside exact spaced-seed hits (enriched for conserved exons, then hard-clamped to `[0.60, 0.72]`), and `gap_open_bits` / `gap_extend_bits` censor all inter-anchor gaps `> 500` bp (`dx <= 500 && dy <= 500` at line 797).
  3. **`horizon_wga --graph` bypasses `estimate_hmm_params`:** `src/bin/horizon_wga.rs:195` (and line 504) calls `align_seq_to_dag_seeded(&dag_nodes, qseq, &HmmParams::default(), 10.0)`, so graph alignment results do not use adaptive `estimate_hmm_params`.
  4. **Homologous-block-only WGA sampling (`scripts/benchmarks/extract_fasta_from_maf.py:28-45`):** The UCSC/Zoonomia WGA benchmarks concatenate only MAF blocks containing both reference and target rows (`if ref_row and tgt_row:`) forced onto `+` strand, stripping unaligned background sequence and inversions.
- **Correction:** Restrict C2 to pairwise `horizon_wga` on pre-extracted homologous-block FASTAs, disclose prefix-4,000-bp anchor sampling and `&HmmParams::default()` in `--graph` mode, and replace prefix `take(64)`/`break` with stratified sampling across all LCBs and strands.

## F6 -- C7: `align_seq_to_dag_seeded` drops seeds in short nodes (`<14`/`<25` bp) and high-copy/low-complexity windows, and over-expands reachability

- **Claim:** C7 (`docs/PANGENOME_SUITE.md:18`)
- **Verdict:** REFUTED
- **Flaw:**
  1. `align_seq_to_dag_seeded` (`src/mdl/dag.rs:471-483`) runs `canonical_spaced_seeds_with_opts(..., 8, false, ry)` per node (`&nd.seq`). It cannot sample seeds spanning `L`-line junctions or any node shorter than `14` bp (`MASK_W10`) or `25` bp (`MASK_RY20`)—which excludes typical SNP/indel bubble nodes in HPRC (mean ~40 bp)—and purges any seed occurring `> 8` times (`src/seeding/mod.rs:136`) or in low-complexity windows (`src/seeding/mod.rs:20-30,102`).
  2. Reachability closure (`src/mdl/dag.rs:503-518`) propagates backward from `seed_hits` to all ancestors, then forward from all activated nodes to all descendants—so a single seed hit activates the entire ancestor cone and all its downstream paths rather than isolating a local DAG component.
  3. The only DAG benchmark (`docs/BENCHMARK_RESULTS.md:65`, 4-node diamond) has `nodes.len() <= 8`, which bypasses seeding completely (`src/mdl/dag.rs:466`).
- **Correction:** Replace per-node seeding with junction-spanning k-mer/minimizer seeding, restrict forward/backward closure to bounded neighborhoods or anchor-to-anchor intervals, and benchmark on a graph with `> 8` nodes.
