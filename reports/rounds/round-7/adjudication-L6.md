## Finding-by-Finding Rulings

### F1 -- C3: Mischaracterization of CRC as offline/fixed (`λ̂ = 10.0`) and invalid exchangeability premise under Angelopoulos et al. (2024)
- **Ruling:** **UPHELD**
- **What survives:** Both legs of the attack are verified directly in the codebase and manuscript:
  1. **Draft contradicts code:** [`manuscript/horizon_paper.md:40`](../../../manuscript/horizon_paper.md#L40) (and Figure 1 caption at [`line 22`](../../../manuscript/horizon_paper.md#L22)) still states *"Using fixed Conformal Risk Control defaults calibrated offline at risk level `α = 0.07` (`n_cal = 15`; Angelopoulos et al. 2024), Horizon enforces a constant bit-savings floor `λ̂ = 10.0` bits (`min_delta_bits = 10.0`)..."*, whereas [`round.json:39`](round.json#L39) and [`src/mdl/mod.rs:428`](../../../src/mdl/mod.rs#L428) run `dp::calibrate_null_crc_for_gc(observed_gc, 15, 0.07, ctx.effective_min_bits, &hmm_params)` dynamically at runtime.
  2. **Invalid attribution to Angelopoulos et al. (2024) (`angelopoulos_2022_conformal`):** Verified in [`src/mdl/dp.rs:82-165`](../../../src/mdl/dp.rs#L82-L165) that `calibrate_null_crc_for_gc` generates `n_cal = 15` synthetic `64`-bp i.i.d. PRNG decoy pairs (`0x9E37_79B9_7F4A_7C15_u64`), which are not exchangeable with real genomic intervals (as [`manuscript/horizon_paper.md:61`](../../../manuscript/horizon_paper.md#L61) itself shows: `4/5` other `18%`-GC `10`-kb random seeds emit `80–448` false-positive pairs and `30%`/`45%`-GC draws emit `288`/`149` pairs). Moreover, at `n_cal = 15` and `α = 0.07`, `(n_ge + 1) / (n_cal + 1) <= 0.07` holds only for `n_ge = 0` (`1/16 = 0.0625 <= 0.07`; `2/16 = 0.125 > 0.07`), reducing `calibrate_crc_threshold` to the maximum of 15 synthetic 64-bp decoy scores (clamped at `base_floor_bits`).
- **Repair & cost:** Updating `manuscript/horizon_paper.md:22, 40` to match the runtime behavior and replacing the Angelopoulos et al. (2024) Conformal Risk Control guarantee claim with an accurate description of the heuristic synthetic-decoy max-floor calibration (`n_cal = 15` 64-bp i.i.d. draws) is **free** (endorsed); actually calibrating CRC on held-out exchangeable biological null intervals is **non-free**.
